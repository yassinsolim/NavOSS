iOS + CarPlay technical beta

Navigation that keeps your trips to yourself.

NavOSS is private, account-free, open-source navigation for iPhone and CarPlay. Search, spoken turn-by-turn guidance and rerouting work without an account, ads or tracking.

Coverage today: Calgary and Kelowna. Alberta and British Columbia are planned and not live yet.

In the app today

Everything you need to get there.

The features below ship in the current technical beta. Where coverage is limited, the app says so instead of guessing.

  • Spoken turn-by-turn

    Voice guidance, clear maneuver banners and automatic rerouting that keep running with the phone locked.

  • CarPlay

    Route preview, guidance and the map on your car's display, sharing one navigation session with the phone.

  • Search places & addresses

    Self-hosted OpenStreetMap search, plus City of Calgary business and address records for Calgary.

  • Route choices

    Compare alternative routes before you go and avoid highways when you'd rather take the long way.

  • Official cameras & road events

    Spoken warnings for City of Calgary intersection safety cameras, and official DriveBC closures and incidents on the map around Kelowna.

  • Places stay on your phone

    Saved places and recent destinations live only on the device, ready on the phone and in CarPlay.

In development

What's being built next.

None of these is in the beta today, and none is switched on in the deployed service. Each ships only after it works on real roads and the privacy policy describes it.

  • In development

    Community road reports

    Mark crashes, closures, hazards and stopped vehicles for other drivers, without an account. The code is built but not switched on in the service, and no released app shares reports. Today the report button only keeps expiring drafts on your phone.

  • In development

    Community traffic

    Free live traffic built from speeds shared by drivers who opt in, shown only where at least three drivers have been. The code is built and off by default; NavOSS shows no live traffic today.

  • In development

    Offline maps

    Download a region and keep the map and routing working without a signal. A bundle pipeline exists, but the on-device part is still in review and nothing is hosted. The beta needs a connection today.

How privacy works

No account. No profile. No trip history.

A navigator has to know where you are. In today's beta, NavOSS uses that only to get you there. Anything new, like opt-in community traffic, is described in the privacy policy before it ships.

Read the privacy policy

  1. Open the app and go

    There is no sign-up, advertising ID, analytics SDK or cross-app tracking in the default build.

  2. Requests are answered, then dropped

    Search and route requests are processed in memory on a server in Alberta. They are not written to a database, access log or backup.

  3. Guidance runs on the phone

    Route matching and camera checks happen on the device. Location updates stop when you end the trip or arrive.

  4. Your places are yours

    Saved and recent destinations stay on the phone. Clear them in one tap, or remove the app.

Open source

Every claim is checkable.

The app, the navigation engine, the API and the operating runbooks are public. So is where the map comes from.
