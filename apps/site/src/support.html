Navigation technical beta

Support and route reports

Use GitHub Issues for reproducible bugs and route-quality reports. The structured forms ask for useful evidence while discouraging private trip data.

NavOSS support is not an emergency service. Follow posted signs and laws. Stop using an unsafe route when it is safe to do so.

Useful report details

  • App version and build number
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • Local date and time
  • Public start area or landmark and destination
  • Expected road or maneuver and actual behavior
  • Whether Avoid highways was enabled
  • Whether Apple Maps or Google Maps differed at that same time

Do not include a home address, precise personal trip history, credential, or unrelated private screenshot.

Known limitations

The capabilities below describe the current TestFlight beta, not a public release.

  • The service covers Canada for search and routing; builds 75 and 80 follow the server's coverage. Build 85 uses it and was distributed to testers on November 23, 2026. Build 70 uses v1 routing for Calgary and Kelowna
  • The service has province-wide road events for Alberta, British Columbia and Ontario; the phone list is in builds 81 and 82, distributed to testers on November 15, 2026, and build 83, distributed on November 17, build 84, distributed on November 20, and build 85, distributed on November 23; builds 75 and 80 use Calgary and Kelowna feeds. 511 Alberta and Ontario 511 were connected in production on November 15, 2026
  • No live traffic or traffic-aware ETA
  • Lane guidance and signposts start with build 75 and depend on what the route provides
  • Offline maps, search and routing are downloadable for All Alberta or Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane & Okotoks in the current TestFlight build. British Columbia has no offline region yet. A physical iPhone download remains unverified
  • No Android release
  • In build 80 and later, sharing road reports is optional and off by default; reports from verified installations are unconfirmed until corroborated. Earlier builds cannot share
  • CarPlay requires a compatible vehicle; complete wired and wireless validation remains outstanding
  • Safety-camera records can change between City updates

Security

Do not disclose a vulnerability publicly. Use GitHub's private vulnerability report.
